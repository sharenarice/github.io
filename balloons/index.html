Bop the balloons, keep them in the sky.

Five brightly colored balloons drift around your screen. Use your head, shoulders, arms — really anything — to keep them aloft. Bop them hard for a louder, brighter little sound.

Best on a laptop

  • Sit back so your shoulders and arms are fully in frame.
  • Make sure your room is well lit.
  • Headphones or speakers on for the bop sounds.
