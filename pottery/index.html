A computer vision toy

Throw a pot with your hands.

The wheel is spinning. Hold your hands in front of your webcam, on either side of the clay, and shape the pot by moving them closer to or further from the center.

Best on a laptop — a wide camera, good light, and a little room behind you to gesture make all the difference.
